1. Information Stored by the Game

Sudoku Grove stores puzzle progress, answers entered, pencil notes, completion times, personal bests, Grove points, shop items, badges, theme choice, sound/vibration preferences, mistake-display choice, language choice, tutorial preference, and public-score sharing choice locally on your device. The app does not operate its own account server or collect personal profiles on a developer server.

Your local game progress file is included in Android’s standard device backup and device-to-device transfer so your progress can move to a new phone. That backup is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, can be turned off in your phone’s settings, and is never sent to the developer.

2. Optional Google Play Games Cloud Save & Leaderboards

You can play the complete game and keep personal bests offline without signing in. If you choose to connect Google Play Games, the app can sync your progress through Google Play Games Saved Games. Public Grove points and Challenge level leaderboards are separate, optional, and gated behind a neutral age screen and an explicit opt-in toggle. Players who select “Under 13 or unsure” cannot access public leaderboards. Google processes Play Games data under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

3. Optional Rewarded Ads & Privacy Choices

Sudoku Grove uses Google Mobile Ads solely for optional rewarded ads — when a player chooses to watch an ad for a hint in Story, Daily, or Puzzle Garden mode, or to respawn after a Challenge timer expires (at most two respawns per run). No forced ads interrupt ordinary play.

Ad requests are configured with child age treatment and a G maximum content rating, and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages required privacy messages and consent status. Where required by law, an ad privacy options button appears in Records → Settings.

4. Optional In-App Purchases

Sudoku Grove offers optional one-time purchases via Google Play Billing (“No ads”, “Premium pack”, and “Open all chapters” upgrade) behind a grown-up check. All payments are handled by Google Play; the developer never receives payment card details. Owned entitlements are cached locally for offline use and re-verified with Google Play on launch.

5. Sharing

When you tap the Share button after completing a puzzle, the app opens Android’s standard share sheet with a plain-text puzzle summary containing no personal information. Nothing is shared automatically.

6. Children and Families

Sudoku Grove is designed for players of all ages. Children can enjoy the entire game without signing into any account. Ad requests use child age treatment and G content rating, public leaderboards are age-gated, and in-app purchases include a grown-up check before opening Google Play’s purchase dialog.

7. Storage and Deletion

You can delete all local game data at any time by clearing Sudoku Grove’s app data or uninstalling the app. Android device backups can be managed in your phone’s Google backup settings. To delete Google Play Games data, follow Google’s Play Games data deletion guide.

8. Changes and Contact

If our data practices change, we will update this privacy policy before the relevant release. For privacy questions, contact the support address listed at the top of this page.
